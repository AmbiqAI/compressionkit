## How to read these results

- **Faithful PRD** compares reconstruction with the recorded input; **Truth PRD** uses the fixture's clean reference. Their difference depends on the input noise and reference construction.
- **PRDN-noise** is a noise-normalized error measure. Interpret it with waveform and physiological errors, not as proof of denoising by itself.
- **HR MAE** and band error describe the measurements in this evaluation. Acceptance thresholds depend on your application.
- **Seam ratio** reports boundary energy relative to the window center. A missing value means no result was reported.
- Sample counts and noise-bucket composition can differ across ratios. Preserve that context when comparing candidates.

See the [validation scorecard](/compressionkit/validation-scorecard/) for definitions and [customer evidence](/compressionkit/customer-evidence/) for a compact comparison.
