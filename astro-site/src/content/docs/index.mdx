## Less data to store. More signal to work with.

compressionKIT is a Python-based AI Development Kit for compressing ECG and PPG waveforms. It brings together codec models, signal preprocessing, evaluation tools, and export utilities so you can compare compression methods using the same workflow.

A **codec** encodes a frame of samples into a smaller representation, then decodes it back into a waveform. Compression is lossy: a smaller payload can also change the reconstructed signal. Use the included scorecards to measure that tradeoff before choosing a configuration.

## Start with a codec. Expand when you need to.

1. **Try a round trip.** [Install from source](/compressionkit/getting-started/) and encode/decode a synthetic frame. You do not need a training dataset.
2. **Evaluate your signal.** Use an [example notebook](/compressionkit/examples/) to inspect reconstructions and errors on your recordings.
3. **Compare configurations.** Browse [PPG and ECG models](/compressionkit/models/) and choose candidates against your quality and payload requirements.
4. **Prepare integration.** [Validate the deploy package](/compressionkit/deployment/#recommended-validation), then measure memory, latency, and quality in your target application.
